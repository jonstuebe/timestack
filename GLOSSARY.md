# Timestack

A daily history game: every player gets the same five events and puts them in chronological order.

## Language

**Puzzle**:
The single daily challenge every player shares: five Events to put in order, plus a Fact.
_Avoid_: Question, daily question, round

**Puzzle number**:
The position of a Puzzle counted from the Launch date, where the Launch date's Puzzle is #1.
_Avoid_: Day number, puzzle id

**Launch date**:
The local calendar date on which Puzzle #1 is played.

**Event**:
A dated historical happening shown as a card inside a Puzzle. Its title never gives away its date.
_Avoid_: Card, item

**Fact**:
The "Did you know?" line attached to a Puzzle, shown after the reveal and never shared.
_Avoid_: Trivia, tidbit
